# ADR-0005 — One prover build per image, from a copy of its tarball kept here

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.11.15
**Chosen by:** the assistant. Whether to keep a copy of the tarball is listed for the custodian in the session
record, §9 item 3. Open to change.
**State:** One prover build per image is built (`tlapm` ff40755). The kept copy and the upstream comparison are
Phase 1.
**Depends on:** ADR-0002.
**Relates to:** `PLAN.md` D1, which is TOPAK's decision and is not made here. ADR-0011 (licences).
**Pending:** a check of the licences of what the tarball bundles (Isabelle, Poly/ML, Z3, Zenon, LS4), before
anything is redistributed.

---

## Context

On 2026.11.15 the three projects were on three prover builds: TOPAK on 7824dab (arm64-darwin), gov-tla on
c8ddc44 (x86_64-linux), and this image on ff40755 (x86_64-linux). Upstream serves only the last (F-5). gov-tla's
pinned tarball can no longer be downloaded.

A newer prover can change what proves. ff40755 proves all 34 of TOPAK's proof modules with the committed
obligation counts (F-3). It does not reproduce TOPAK's defect record. Of the lemmas the record expects to prove,
13 fail, the false statements among them (F-7). That fits upstream having closed the prover's two unsound cases
since 7824dab. The upstream issues were not read, so this is an inference.

The tarball is 52 MB (F-6).

## Decision

1. **An image carries exactly one `tlapm` build,** with the backends its tarball bundles. A project that moves to
   a new image moves its prover pin, knowingly.
2. **tla-kit keeps a copy of each tarball it pins,** as a release asset of this repository, so that the image
   can be rebuilt after upstream has moved the tag. The `Dockerfile` builds from the copy.
3. **A separate check compares the copy with what upstream serves,** and reports the result. It does not fail the
   build. It is the analogue of TOPAK's `sources.yml`: it says when upstream has moved, and the move is then a
   decision.
4. **A prover move here needs two runs first:** the full proof run over every project the image serves
   (`spike/prove-all.sh`, or its Phase 1 successor), and TOPAK's defect run. The two results are recorded with the
   new pin.
5. **tla-kit does not decide a project's pin.** Whether TOPAK or gov-tla moves to the image's prover is decided in
   that project (D1). What tla-kit provides is the image, the two runs above, and the kept tarball.

## Consequences

- Rebuilding a published image no longer depends on upstream.
- tla-kit becomes a redistributor of Isabelle, Z3, Zenon, LS4 and Poly/ML. Item 2 waits on the licence check
  above.
- A project that moves its pin regenerates its proof results files. For TOPAK that is 34 files, and the records
  that cite the two unsound cases need reading first. That cost is the project's, and is why item 5 leaves the
  choice to the project.
- Until item 2 is done, a rebuild can fail on the checksum by design (ADR-0002).

## Alternatives not taken

- **Several prover builds in one image, chosen by a variable.** Every project could keep its own pin, but the
  image's digest would no longer say which prover produced a result. The projects' builds are also not all
  served any more.
- **Rely on upstream to keep old tarballs.** It does not (F-5).
- **Build `tlapm` from source at a commit.** That is the route for arm64 (ADR-0003). For x86_64 it adds a
  toolchain and a build nobody has checked, against a binary that proves everything (F-3).
