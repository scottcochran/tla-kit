# ADR-0017 — cvc5 and ethos are built from copies kept here, in one release with their copyleft parts' source

**Status:** Accepted · 2026.11.17
**Chosen by:** the custodian chose to keep the two zips, and option A of the licence check, on 2026.11.17, and
chose that `spec/PinLifecycle.tla` models them (item 5). The rest of the form below is the assistant's.
**State:** Planned, Phase 6. Nothing is kept yet.
**Depends on:** ADR-0005 (the pattern), ADR-0012 (item 8), ADR-0013 (items 2, 4 and 8).
**Relates to:** ADR-0011 (the notice), ADR-0014 (releases are never deleted), ADR-0015 (`spec/PinLifecycle.tla`).
**Pending:** none.

---

## Context

ADR-0012 item 8 puts `cvc5` 1.4.0, `ethos` 0.2.4 and cvc5 1.4.0's CPC signature in the base. The licence check found
that both binaries link LGPL libraries statically. Its option A, which the custodian chose, redistributes
upstream's builds on D7's three conditions:
1. the notices go with them;
2. a kept release offers the copyleft parts' source;
3. the linked releases are settled from the bytes.

Condition 3 is met: glibc `2.35-0ubuntu3.14` in both, GMP 6.3.0, MPFR 4.2.2 and LibPoly 0.2.1 in `cvc5`, and
Ubuntu's GMP 6.2.1 in `ethos`.

The custodian also chose to keep the two zips themselves. ADR-0013 item 8 says that of the image's downloads only
the prover's is kept here, and that keeping another needs a record. This is that record. The prover's tarball is
the precedent: upstream stopped serving it, and the copy in `tlapm-ff40755` is what the image builds from.

`ethos` checks proofs against the CPC signature of the `cvc5` that wrote them. TOPAK pins the `ethos` commit that
cvc5 1.4.0 names, and the signature from the same tag. So the three move together.

## Decision

1. **tla-kit keeps `cvc5`'s and `ethos`'s zips, in one release of their own,** tagged `cvc5-<version>`, for
   example `cvc5-1.4.0`. It is made before the pull request that pins them, by a script like
   `tools/keep-tlapm.sh`. A pull request may name only zips already kept. The release holds:
   - `cvc5-Linux-x86_64-static.zip` and `ethos-linux-x86_64.zip`, as upstream served them;
   - `licenses/` and `NOTICE` (condition 1);
   - the source of each copyleft part, at the release in the binaries, by the table of
     `derived/tla-kit-static-libraries-2026.11.17.md` §4 (condition 2);
   - cvc5's source at its tag, which holds the CPC signature, and ethos's source at its tag;
   - `SHA256SUMS` over all of them.

   Every download is checked against a digest the script names, and the script stops on any difference.
2. **The `Dockerfile` installs them from the kept copies,** in CI and in the release workflow alike, as it does
   the prover (ADR-0005 item 2). The CPC signature comes from cvc5's kept source.
3. **The weekly check compares each kept zip with what upstream serves** (ADR-0013 item 4) and reports the result,
   without failing the build, as for the prover (ADR-0005 item 3).
4. **`cvc5`, `ethos` and the CPC signature move together, in one pull request,** as the base and the `apt`
   snapshot do (ADR-0013 item 2). Their evidence is a checker's (ADR-0013 item 3): the self-test's check of
   ADR-0012 item 8, and TOPAK's certified step in the candidate image. A move makes a new kept release first.
5. **`spec/PinLifecycle.tla` models them.** It gains a fourth pin, `smt`, for the three that move together
   (item 4): a checker pin, with a kept copy, whose upstream is not taken to keep what it serves. Its
   `BuildsFromKept` covers every kept pin. The bounds of ADR-0015 item 5 grow from three pins to four by a History
   row there. This record is accepted only when every row has the outcome it expects, as ADR-0015 item 7 asks of
   ADR-0005, ADR-0013 and ADR-0014.

## Consequences

- Two more upstreams are relied on only for the first download: `cvc5`'s and `ethos`'s GitHub releases. A
  release built from a kept copy can be rebuilt after upstream deletes an asset.
- The kept release grows with the copyleft source, glibc's above all, at about 18 MB. It is made once per move.
- On acceptance, ADR-0013 gains a History row: item 8 names this record beside ADR-0005, and item 2 names the
  three pins of item 4 as one move. `PINS.json` gives each of them a `kept_release`.
- The model grows with the fourth pin. A run of every row takes longer, in CI on every pull request that touches
  `spec/` or `decisions/` (ADR-0015 item 8).

## Alternatives not taken

- **Point to upstream's source and keep nothing.** It costs nothing now. But it relies on upstream continuing to
  serve the source, and the GPL-3.0 §6 obligation to keep the source available stays with tla-kit. cvc5 builds
  GMP and MPFR from a branch of `cvc5/cvc5-deps`, not a fixed commit.
- **Keep the source but not the zips.** It meets condition 2, and a rebuild after upstream deletes a zip would
  then fail. The custodian chose to keep them.
- **One release for each tool.** The three move together (item 4), so one release names the set.
