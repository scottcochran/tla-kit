# ADR-0018 — tla-kit is the home of the method and of the coupling, not only of the image

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.11.20
**Chosen by:** the custodian chose that the coupling lives here (in session, 2026.11.20). The assistant chose the
form below.
**State:** Planned. Nothing below is built. ADR-0019 to ADR-0023 are drafted with it.
**Depends on:** ADR-0001.
**Relates to:** ADR-0007 (superseded by ADR-0022 if both are accepted), ADR-0015 and ADR-0016 (the habit this record
generalizes), ADR-0019 to ADR-0023 (what it brings here).
**Pending:** none.

---

## Context

ADR-0001 makes tla-kit one image of the TLA+ tool family with two ways in. Since then tla-kit has also become the
one place where the whole chain runs on itself: records whose items name the actions of its modules (ADR-0015 item
2), results made only by the skill's generator, CI that reruns them and blocks a merge on failure (ADR-0014 item 1),
evidence over the projects it serves (ADR-0013 item 3), an agent job checked apart from the agent (`agent.yml`), and
a person who accepts every record.

The method that chain runs on lives elsewhere. The skill's source is in TOPAK and vendored here (ADR-0007). gov-tla
carries a hand copy. The records form exists only as a convention of `decisions/` and as TOPAK's project-specific
skills. Three repositories keep up by hand.

`COUPLING.md` sets out what is coupled today, what is prose, and what would make the rest data.

## Decision

1. **tla-kit holds four things:** the image (ADR-0001); the method, as the skills that drive it (ADR-0022); the
   coupling, as the claims table and the claims check (ADR-0019, ADR-0020); and the CI that enforces it
   (ADR-0021).
2. **ADR-0001 item 3 binds all four.** Nothing here is specific to one project. TOPAK's genesis, Batches,
   confirmation markers and checkpoints stay TOPAK's, as a profile over what tla-kit provides.
3. **tla-kit uses each of them on itself first.** A part is offered to the projects it serves only once tla-kit's
   own records, modules and CI pass it.
4. **A project takes as much as it wants,** at the levels of `COUPLING.md` §3.5: a pin of the image; results
   compared in the image; a claims table behind a required check; a typed graph of every edge. Only the first is
   asked of a project listed in `CONSUMERS.md`.

## Consequences

- `README.md` and `PLAN.md`'s Goal describe tla-kit as the image only. Both change if this is accepted.
- The method's releases and the image's become one line of releases (ADR-0022).
- tla-kit's maintenance grows by the skills and the check. Its one custodian is the same as TOPAK's and gov-tla's,
  which `COUPLING.md` §7 names as the limit dogfooding does not lift.

## Alternatives not taken

- **Keep the method in TOPAK.** TOPAK is the heaviest user and a design corpus in its own right; its records about
  itself would keep governing a tool every project uses.
- **A third repository for the method.** It would add one more pin to keep in step, where the point is to remove one.
