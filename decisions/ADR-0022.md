# ADR-0022 — The skills' source moves here, and is released with the image under one tag

**Status:** Proposed · 2026.11.20
**Chosen by:** the assistant, following the custodian's choice in ADR-0018. `COUPLING.md` §6 E4 and E6 are the
custodian's to settle; the record takes the recommendations.
**State:** Planned. The skill's scripts are vendored from TOPAK at `6a26310` (`vendor/VENDOR-PIN`).
**Depends on:** ADR-0018.
**Relates to:** ADR-0007 (superseded by this record when it is accepted), ADR-0011 (licences), ADR-0014 (releases),
ADR-0019 (the form the records skill writes).
**Pending:** on acceptance, ADR-0007's status becomes Superseded by ADR-0022, by a History row.

---

## Context

The method lives in TOPAK; tla-kit vendors its scripts by digest; gov-tla copies `SKILL.md` by hand and its README
asks that it be copied again when it changes. A fix travels TOPAK first, then a vendoring pull request here, then a
copy there. And there is no records skill outside TOPAK, whose three are about its own genesis.

## Decision

1. **The `writing-tla-specs-and-proofs` skill's source moves to `skills/writing-tla-specs-and-proofs/`,** with its
   scripts and fixture, at the TOPAK commit `vendor/VENDOR-PIN` names. `vendor/` is removed. ADR-0007 items 3 and 4
   carry over: CI checks the scripts the image installs against the skill's tree by digest, and the skill stays
   usable without Docker.
2. **A records skill joins it,** `writing-decision-records`, drawn from this repository's `decisions/` form: the
   header lines, History rows, accepted text never edited, claim ids on checked items (ADR-0019 item 4). It drafts
   and revises. It never accepts.
3. **One tag releases the image and the plugin.** A plugin's `.mcp.json` names the image of its own release, by
   digest. Nobody pairs them by hand.
4. **Projects consume the skills by their tla-kit pin,** and move to a new release by their own decision. TOPAK's
   `skills/writing-tla-specs-and-proofs` stops being the source, by a TOPAK record; TOPAK's own records skills keep
   what is TOPAK's and take the shared form from item 2.
5. **The licences stay:** Apache-2.0 for scripts, CC BY 4.0 for the skills' prose (ADR-0011 item 5).

## Consequences

- A fix to the method is one pull request here, not two in two repositories.
- TOPAK's and gov-tla's skill copies change by their own pull requests, asked for there.
- The plugin's version number becomes the image's (ADR-0014 item 2's scheme), and a method-only change takes a PATCH.

## Alternatives not taken

- **Keep vendoring, the other way round.** TOPAK would vendor from here. It keeps two copies in step by digest where
  one is enough.
- **The records skill first.** It would have no check to pass; it follows ADR-0020.
